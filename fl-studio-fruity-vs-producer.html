The big difference

Audio recording — why it matters

The biggest difference between Fruity and Producer Edition is audio recording and audio clips in the playlist:

  • Fruity Edition: you can only use virtual instruments (synthesisers, drum machines). No recording of live vocals, guitar, piano, or any real instrument.
  • Producer Edition: you can record audio directly into the playlist, chop and arrange audio clips, layer vocals over your beats and use Edison to edit recordings.
If you ever plan to record vocals, record a live instrument, or work with audio samples in the playlist — you need Producer Edition. The $100 difference is worth it.
Who should choose Fruity?

When Fruity Edition makes sense

  • You are on a very tight budget and only plan to use virtual instruments
  • You produce purely electronic music with no live recordings
  • You are a complete beginner just wanting to learn the basics
  • You plan to upgrade to Producer later (Image-Line allows paid upgrades)
Even if you think you do not need audio recording now, consider that FL Studio includes lifetime free updates. You will likely want audio clips at some point. Producer Edition is the better long-term investment.
FAQ

Fruity vs Producer questions

Can I upgrade from Fruity to Producer?
Yes. Log in to your Image-Line account at image-line.com and purchase the upgrade. You pay the price difference ($100). Your existing licence is upgraded in-place.
FL Studio fruity edition vs producer edition 2026 — which is better?
Producer Edition is better for most people. The $100 difference adds audio recording, audio clips, automation clips and Edison — all features you will likely want eventually. Fruity is only worth choosing if you are strictly on a budget and know you will never record live audio.
Does Fruity Edition have the full piano roll?
Yes. Both Fruity and Producer Edition have the full MIDI piano roll. The difference is not in MIDI capabilities but in audio recording and clips. Fruity can do complex MIDI arrangements; it just cannot record or play back audio clips.
