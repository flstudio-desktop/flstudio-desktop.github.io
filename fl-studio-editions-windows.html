Fruity vs Producer

The main difference: audio recording

The key upgrade from Fruity to Producer is audio recording and audio clips in the playlist. If you want to:

  • Record vocals, guitar, or any live instrument → you need Producer Edition
  • Chop and arrange audio samples freely in the playlist → you need Producer Edition
  • Use automation clips for complex parameter changes → you need Producer Edition

If you only produce with virtual instruments and do not need audio recording, Fruity Edition works. But for $100 more, Producer Edition is a significant upgrade.

FAQ

Edition questions

Can I upgrade from Fruity to Producer later?
Yes. Image-Line allows upgrades between editions. You pay the price difference. Log in to your Image-Line account, go to the shop and choose the upgrade option. Your existing licence key is upgraded in-place.
What is included in the FL Studio free trial?
The free trial is the full All Plugins Edition with one restriction: you cannot reopen saved projects. All features, all plugins, all instruments work. You can export audio. This lets you fully evaluate FL Studio before buying. See free trial guide.
FL Studio editions comparison 2026 — which is best value?
Producer Edition ($199) is the best value for most producers. Signature Bundle ($299) adds Gross Beat and Harmor which many producers use regularly. All Plugins ($499) is worth it only if you actively use most Image-Line instruments — Sytrus, Harmless, Morphine, etc.
FL Studio fruity edition vs producer edition differences 2026
The main differences: Producer Edition adds audio recording (vocals, guitar), audio clips in the playlist, automation clips and the Edison audio editor. Fruity Edition is limited to 64 instrument tracks and has no audio recording capability.
