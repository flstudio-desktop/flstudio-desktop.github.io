Why not crack

Why cracked FL Studio is a bad idea

  • Malware risk — cracked DAW software is one of the most common malware delivery vectors. The "crack" executable is often a trojan or spyware. Music producers have lost project files, passwords and banking information this way
  • No updates — cracked versions are frozen at whatever version was cracked. You miss all performance fixes, bug patches and new features
  • Instability — crack patches modify the executable in ways that often cause crashes, plugin incompatibilities and corrupted projects
  • No support — if something goes wrong, you cannot contact Image-Line support
  • Pointless — the official free trial has no time limit and no feature restrictions. There is no reason to crack FL Studio
